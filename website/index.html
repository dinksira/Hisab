Direct Install · Android Only

On your phone in
under 60 seconds.

No Play Store. No Google account. No waiting for review. Just download, allow, install, done.

01

Download the APK

Tap the button below on your Android phone. The file is called Hisab.apk (~172 MB).

Download Hisab.apk
02

Allow installs from unknown sources

Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store by default. You need to allow this once.

Android 10 – 14
  1. Open Settings
  2. Go to Apps → Special app access
  3. Tap Install unknown apps
  4. Select your browser or Files app
  5. Toggle Allow from this source
Samsung One UI
  1. Open Settings
  2. Go to Biometrics and security
  3. Tap Install unknown apps
  4. Select My Files or your browser
  5. Toggle Allow from this source
You can re-disable this after installing Hisab for extra security.
03

Open the downloaded file

Open your Downloads folder and tap Hisab.apk.

📁 Can't find it?

Open your file manager (My Files on Samsung, Files on Pixel), then navigate to Downloads.

04

Tap Install

Android will show a confirmation dialog. Review permissions, then tap Install. Takes about 5–10 seconds.

05

Open Hisab — you're done

Tap Open from the installer or find Hisab in your app drawer. No sign-up required.

✅ You're in Demo Mode

Hisab launches with realistic pre-seeded accounts, transactions, and budgets. Explore everything before adding your real data.

The name

Why Hisab?

حساب is the Arabic word for account, calculation, and reckoning. All three meanings were intentional.

Managing money is not just tracking numbers. It is an act of reckoning — an honest accounting of how you live, what you value, and where you are going.

Hisab was built because most budgeting apps are either too simple to be useful or too complex to be used. It occupies the precise middle ground: engineered for correctness, designed for clarity.
